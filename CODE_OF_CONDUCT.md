# Code of conduct

This project welcomes contributors of every background and experience level.

- Be respectful and constructive. Critique code, not people.
- Assume good intent; ask before assuming.
- No harassment, discrimination or personal attacks, in any form.
- Respect privacy: never share personal data of applicants or staff, including in issues and screenshots.

Problems can be reported privately to the maintainer ([@brittytino](https://github.com/brittytino)).
Maintainers may remove comments, commits or contributors that do not follow this code.
